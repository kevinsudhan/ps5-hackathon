Every order is a promise.

Aisha Rahman in Liverpool was promised her order by December 14. On October 20, a cyber incident shuts the terminals at the Port of Singapore (Oct 20–24, then 3 days of backlog). Her parcel never goes near Singapore. It will still be late.

02 · The graph

One FalkorDB graph per company: ports, lanes, shipments that feed each other, orders and their promises

A closed port is a starting node. The damage is a path, and one Cypher query walks it.

MATCH (e:Event {id: $event})-[:AFFECTS]->(h:Hub) MATCH p = (s0)-[:FEEDS*0..8]->(s:Shipment) WITH s, reduce(d = d0, f IN relationships(p) | CASE WHEN d > f.buffer_days THEN d - f.buffer_days ELSE 0 END) AS delay MATCH (s)<-[:FULFILLED_BY]-(o:Order)-[:HAS_PROMISE]->(pr:Promise) RETURN pr.id, late, late * pr.penalty_per_day
